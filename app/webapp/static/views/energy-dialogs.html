        Solar boost

        Units enter and leave boost one at a time, so their combined draw never outruns the surplus.

        The next unit waits a full settle interval, then needs the boost entry threshold plus the margin still measured spare. Sustained import above the fast-shed value drops every boost at once; otherwise they leave one at a time, most recent first. Units are admitted in a fixed order by unit id.

        Sun-position check

        Measured performance by hour against the sun's direction. A dip at the same direction every day is shading; one that moves with the clock is weather.
